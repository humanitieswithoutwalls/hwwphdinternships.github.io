This an example of a program timeline that the Illinois Bridge Program followed in 2026. 

•	January 28: Info Session
•	February 27: Applications due
•	March 9: Selection Committee Meeting
•	March 12: Notify students
•	March 27: Deadline for students to accept offer (2 weeks to account for spring break)
•	April 3: Deadline for students to complete the project matching form (Sent to students by campus collaborator Community Learning Lab (CLL))
•	April 3-13: 
o	  CLL identifies partners that match students’ interests and communicates with students on their preferences
•	April 13-May 15: CLL starts outreach to community organizations 
•	April 24: Orientation
•	May 1-May 15: 
o	  Community partner-student introductions go out on rolling basis as matches are confirmed
o	  Students meet with partners and lay groundwork for the summer
•	May 17: Students begin their service projects
•	May 24: Summary of Expectations document due to Emily Clark
•	July 18: Students finish their service projects
•	July 18-August 1: 
o	  Community partner survey/feedback survey is active
