## *Timeline*

This an example of a program timeline that the Illinois Bridge Program followed in 2026. 

*	January 28: Info Session
*	February 27: Applications due
*	March 9: Selection Committee Meeting
*	March 12: Notify students
*	March 27: Deadline for students to accept offer (2 weeks to account for spring break)
*	April 3: Deadline for students to complete the project matching form (Sent to students by campus collaborator Community Learning Lab (CLL))
*	April 3-13: CLL identifies partners that match students’ interests and communicates with students on their preferences
* April 13-May 15: CLL starts outreach to community organizations 
*	April 24: Orientation
*	May 1-May 15: Community partner-student introductions go out on rolling basis as matches are confirmed and students meet with partners and lay groundwork for the summer
*	May 17: Students begin their service projects
*	May 22: First student cohort meeting
*	May 24: Summary of Expectations and Media Release documents due
*	June 3: Second student cohort meeting
*	June 15: Halfway checkpoint surveys are sent to students and community partners
*	June 15: Questions for web profile features are sent to students (and are posted throughout the remainder of the program, as students finish (we posted 2 per week))
*	June 18: Third student cohort meeting
*	July 2: Fourth student cohort meeting
*	July 17: Students finish their service projects; final student cohort meeting
*	July 18-August 1: Community partner survey/student feedback surveys are active
