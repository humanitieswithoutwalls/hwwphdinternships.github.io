**Eligibility**

Program eligibility may vary depending on the aim and scope of different graduate internship opportunities. Considerations for student eligibility should include type and discipline of graduate program, stage of progress through degree requirements, enrollment status vis-à-vis internship appointment, projected graduation date, work eligibility/visa status, residency, and others. In the example of the HWW Summer Bridge Program, eligibilty is limited to humanities PhD students (both pre- and ABD) who are enrolled the semester prior to summer work, who plan to graduate no sooner than the semester following the summer term, and who reside near enough to campus to conduct in-person work at a local organization. 

**Timeline**

To recruit applicants for the HWw Summer Bridge Program, publicity begins late in the fall semester. The application is posted to the website in October/November and our communications staff include it in weekly newsletters from then until the applications are due, along with a link to register for an info, which is held in January.
