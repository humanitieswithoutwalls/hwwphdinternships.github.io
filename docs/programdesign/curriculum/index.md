# **Curriculum**

Secondary to the internship, the other component of the summer program is regular cohort meetings throughout the summer. The Summer Bridge program holds these every two weeks and students share what they have been working on, what they're learning, and bring up any challenges or issues they're facing. This provides the students with the space to hear from their peers, learn about their experiences, and collectively troubleshoot. These sessions are also times to dive into professional development topics. You may choose to set a curriculum for the program, or solicit input from the students about what they'd like to cover. Topics may include:
* Representing their internship and other community engaged work in resumes and CVs;
* Career diversity and exploring options outside of academia;
* Responsible, ethical, and equitable community engaged research;
* A deeper dive into reciprocity and redistribution methodologically;
* Cultivating community partnerships;
* Community engaged teaching and service learning course development;
* And more, depending on the students' interests and career goals, and the types of organizations they are partnered with.
